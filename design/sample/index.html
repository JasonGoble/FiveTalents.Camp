How to test a printer

  1. Open a sample and choose Print. Set scale to 100% (not "fit to page"), margins to None, and turn on Background graphics so the header bands print.
  2. Print it in colour on the inkjet and on the laser.
  3. Print it again in greyscale or black and white, or photocopy the colour print.
  4. Check: every word is readable (nothing lighter than mid-grey), rules are visible, and every status can still be read as a word.
  5. For the cards, check the cut size against your card stock or badge holder.
