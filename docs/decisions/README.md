# Architecture Decision Records

ADRs capture the why behind significant decisions. Each is numbered and never edited after acceptance. A reversed decision gets a new ADR that references the old one.

## Index

| # | Title | Status |
|---|-------|--------|

## Template

```markdown
# ADR-NNNN: Title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](XXXX-title.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context
Why was a decision needed? What forces were at play?

## Decision
What we decided.

## Consequences
What gets easier, what gets harder.
```
